Measured on the plug the inverter is on, which is the only instrument that can see this plant at all - it is not on the house inverter's MPPT inputs, so none of this appears in the PV figure anywhere else on these pages. A gap is a gap, not a zero: the plug stops being asked when it stops answering.

Where it went

Every kilowatt-hour this made either displaced one the house was about to buy or went out through the meter. Which of the two it was is decided per sample against what the connection point was doing at that moment, and the difference is worth more than twice as much per kWh - so it is measured rather than assumed.
