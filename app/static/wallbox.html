Charging Power

Two readings of the same thing. The charger's own register goes quiet while a car draws, because reading it would cut the current; the clamp on its feed does not. Gaps are drawn as gaps rather than as zero, which would claim the car was plugged in and taking nothing.

Three witnesses

The same energy, measured three ways: the clamp meter on the charger's feed, the charger's own power register, and the charger's own lifetime counter. Whether they agree is a question about the record, so it is answered from the record - and each line says what it does and does not establish.

Where the charging came from

Each day's charging split by what was supplying the house at the moment it flowed: straight off the roof, out of the battery, or bought. Pro rata on the whole supply - no rule for deciding which kWh the car got is a measurement, and this is the convention the rest of the pages use.

Against a petrol car

The measured kilowatt-hours turned into kilometres, and those into the litres a petrol car would have burnt covering them. How far a kWh goes depends on the month - cabin heating is most of the difference - so the line is the whole year and the dots are the months that are in the record.
